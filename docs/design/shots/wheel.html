🎯 Space wheel

Hold. Nudge.
Let go.

A pie of every space, like a ping wheel in a game. Slack, Claude and Notion sit on top, a flick up away. Pick by direction, not distance, so a small flick is enough.

🎡
Mouse: hold the scroll wheel down, nudge toward a space, let go. The last highlighted space opens.
⌥
Trackpad: hold Option, glide your finger, let go of Option.
👆
Quick click keeps it open: click a slice to go there.
🔢
1–8 pick directly. Esc or letting go in the middle cancels.
